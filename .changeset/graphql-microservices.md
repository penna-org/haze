New optional packages. `@hazejs/graphql`: schema-first and code-first GraphQL on graphql-js, GraphQL over HTTP, subscriptions over `graphql-transport-ws`, request-scoped loaders, depth / complexity limits, auth-aware context and GraphiQL. `@hazejs/microservices`: request/reply and events between services over memory, Redis, NATS and gRPC, with handlers discovered from `app/message` and `haze microservice:listen`.
