# Code of Conduct

hazejs adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing others' private information are not acceptable, in any project space — issues, pull requests, discussions and chat.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately through the [penna-org](https://github.com/penna-org) organization. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
